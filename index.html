Notes on pain, patience, and the anxious mind that comes with them.

I started writing this after years of living with sciatica, tight muscles, and the anxiety that seems to feed off both. This is where I keep what I've learned, what's helped, and what hasn't.

What I write about

Three threads that keep showing up in my own recovery, and in what I hear from readers.

  • Sciatica

    What actually eases nerve pain day to day, what I wish I'd known earlier, and the slow work of figuring out what set mine off in the first place.

  • Muscle pain

    Tight hips, sore backs, the muscles that guard an old injury long after it's healed, and gentle ways to loosen them without making things worse.

  • Anxiety

    How chronic pain and anxious thoughts tend to feed each other, and small, unglamorous habits that have helped me interrupt that loop.
